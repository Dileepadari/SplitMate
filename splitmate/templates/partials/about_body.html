About {{ app_name }}

A shared-expense tracker that started as a hackathon project and grew a proper ledger.

What it does

Every expense in a group records who paid and how the cost was divided. From that, {{ app_name }} derives one number per person: what they put in, minus what they used up, minus anything already paid back. A positive number means the group owes them.

Turning those numbers into actual payments is the interesting part. Rather than asking everyone to repay every expense individually, {{ app_name }} matches the biggest debtor against the biggest creditor until nothing is left. That needs at most one payment per person instead of one per expense.

Splitting is never allowed to lose money. Dividing 100 three ways gives 33.34, 33.33 and 33.33, not three amounts that quietly add up to 99.99.
